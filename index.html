⛰️ Mountain Highway Safety Rules & Protocols

• Downhill Descent: Use low gear compression; avoid riding brakes to prevent brake fade on steep inclines.
• Landslide Precautions: Watch for small falling pebbles or muddy runoff—these precede major debris slides.
• High Altitude Sela Pass (NH-13): Carry wheel snow chains; avoid mountain transit between 18:00 and 06:00 during sub-zero freeze.
• Single Lane CRAWL: In flooded or sinking sections of NH-37 Jiribam Gorge, maintain a minimum 50m vehicle gap.
